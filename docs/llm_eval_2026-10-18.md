# Local model eval on the Spark, 2026-10-18

Two candidates for the served model on the Spark, measured with
`scripts/llm_eval.py` under one protocol (KV cache 4 GiB, `--max-model-len 32768`,
`--gpu-memory-utilization 0.80`, 22 cases of `data/llm_eval_cases.json` x 3 repeats,
max_tokens 8192, reasoning_effort low). The window ran 22:34-23:53 Europe/Istanbul with
no other LLM up; the candidates' compose project and the procedure live in the stack
repository (`compose.eval.yml`, `docs/llm-eval.md`). The result files beside this report
(`llm_eval_2026-10-18_*.json`) are the script's own output with the host name and the
home directory replaced.

Cases a candidate did not get exactly right every time: none for either.

How to read the two speed columns: tok/s is end to end per request, reasoning included;
the 35B-A3B writes about six times more tokens per answer than the 27B, so its median
request takes longer (15.4 s against 9.4 s) although it generates much faster. For short
extraction answers the 27B returns sooner; for long answers or several requests at once
the 35B-A3B is the faster one, and its KV cache of 4 GiB holds 170,738 tokens against the
27B's 56,661 (5.2x against 1.7x concurrency at 32k tokens per request).

Other facts from the window:

- Baseline with no LLM: MemAvailable 109.1 GiB (`llm_eval_2026-10-18_baseline.json`).
- The 35B-A3B was healthy 280 s after `up` (weights loaded in 135 s), the 27B after 360 s
  (118 s). Both vLLM v0.31.0 logs warn `Your GPU does not have native support for FP4
  computation but FP4 quantization is being used` and fall back to weight-only FP4
  (Marlin); the numbers above are measured with that path.
- The cgroup CUDA check (`llm_eval_2026-10-18_cgroup_cuda_check.json`): a job under
  `research_run.sh --memory 1G` held 2 GiB on the GPU while its cgroup was charged
  0.09 GiB of it. Verdict `cuda_outside_cgroup`: the launcher's MemoryMax does not bound
  GPU memory, so `--gpu-budget` is the only guard for GPU use (`docs/spark_runbook.md`
  section 7.10).

## The third candidate, and why it is not in the table

The production model until this day, `Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-NVFP4`, was to be measured first.
Its eval start failed twice within a minute: vLLM wants 0.92 of the device's 121.7 GiB
(112 GiB) free at startup, and with the operator's desktop session and the stack's other
containers up the device reports about 106 GiB free (`Free memory on device cuda:0
(105.9/121.69 GiB) on startup is less than desired GPU memory utilization`). The
production service carries the same check. By the account holder's decision of
2026-10-18 that model is no longer started on the Spark; the two candidates above are the
models of this workspace, and the eval compose declares the 0.80 share for them (their
footprint is 32 GiB). Which of the two becomes the served model, and on which port, is
decided after this report; the stack's `docker-compose.yml` changes only then.
